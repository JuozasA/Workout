# EXERCISE GUIDE

## Barbell Bench Press

1. Lie flat on bench, feet planted on floor
2. Grip bar slightly wider than shoulder width
3. Unrack, lower bar to mid-chest
4. Press up explosively, lock out at top
5. Keep shoulder blades pinched throughout

## Deadlift

1. Stand with feet hip-width, bar over mid-foot
2. Hinge at hips, grip bar just outside knees
3. Chest up, back flat, brace core
4. Drive through floor, extend hips and knees together
5. Lock out at top, squeeze glutes
6. Lower with control, hinge at hips first

## Barbell Squat

1. Bar on upper traps, feet shoulder-width
2. Brace core, break at hips and knees together
3. Descend until thighs parallel (or below)
4. Drive through heels, stand explosively
5. Keep knees tracking over toes

## Romanian Deadlift (RDL)

1. Start standing, bar at hips, slight knee bend
2. Hinge at hips — push butt back
3. Lower bar along thighs, feel hamstring stretch
4. Stop around mid-shin (don't touch floor)
5. Drive hips forward to stand, squeeze glutes

## Bulgarian Split Squat

1. Rear foot elevated on bench behind you
2. DB in each hand or goblet hold
3. Lower until front thigh parallel
4. Keep front knee over ankle
5. Drive through front heel to stand

## Cable Woodchop

1. Stand side-on to cable
2. Hips initiate, torso follows, arms stay connected
3. Rotate diagonally across body
4. Control the return — don't let cable snap back
5. High-to-low: cable at top, chop down
6. Low-to-high: cable at bottom, lift up

## 90/90 Hip Switches

1. Sit on floor, one leg in front bent 90°, one leg to side bent 90°
2. Rotate hips to switch legs to opposite position
3. Keep chest tall throughout

## Farmers Walk

1. Heavy DB in each hand at sides
2. Stand tall, shoulders back, core braced
3. Walk with controlled steps
4. Don't let weights swing or pull you forward

# PROGRESSIVE OVERLOAD

Each week, aim for:

* +1-2 reps at same weight
* +2.5kg when you hit top of rep range
* Track every lift — weights and reps
* Increase HIIT sprint speed by 0.5 km/h when it feels easy

---

# NUTRITION TARGETS

For fat loss while building lean muscle:

| Metric | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Calories | ~2,200-2,400 kcal/day |
| Protein | 130-180g (1.6-2g per kg bodyweight) |
| Deficit | 400-500 kcal/day |
| Water | 2.5-3L/day |

**Protein timing:**
* Pre-workout: Light meal 1-2 hours before
* Post-workout: 30-40g protein within 1 hour
* Spread protein across 4-5 meals
