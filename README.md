# EXERCISE GUIDE

## 90/90 Hip Switches

1. Sit on floor, one leg in front bent 90°, one leg to side bent 90°
2. Rotate hips to switch legs to opposite position
3. Keep chest tall throughout

## Dead Bug

1. Lie on back, arms straight up, knees bent 90° over hips
2. Lower opposite arm and leg slowly to floor
3. Keep lower back pressed into the ground throughout
4. Return and switch sides

## Thread the Needle

1. Start on all fours
2. Take one arm, reach under your body and rotate
3. Follow hand with eyes, feel thoracic stretch
4. Return and reach to ceiling

## Open Book Stretch

1. Lie on side, knees bent, arms stacked in front
2. Rotate top arm open across body to other side
3. Follow hand with eyes, keep knees stacked
4. Hold briefly at end range, return

## Cable Pallof Press

1. Stand side-on to cable, handle at chest
2. Press straight out, hold 2 sec
3. Resist the rotation — core stays tight
4. Return to chest

## Half-Kneeling Cable Rotation

1. Kneel with inside knee down, outside foot forward
2. Hold cable handle at chest
3. Rotate torso away from cable
4. Hips stay completely still — only torso moves

## Goblet Squat to Rotation

1. Hold DB at chest, squat down
2. Stand up, rotate torso to one side
3. Return to centre, squat, rotate other side

## Hollow Body Hold

1. Lie on back, arms overhead, legs straight
2. Lift shoulders and legs off floor, lower back pressed into ground
3. Hold the "banana" shape — anti-extension stability
4. Breathe normally, don't let lower back arch

## Slow Motion Swing Drill

1. Golf posture, arms crossed on chest
2. Backswing: rotate torso while keeping hips quiet
3. Pause at top
4. Downswing: hips fire first, then torso, then arms
5. Exaggerate the sequence — hips THEN torso THEN arms

# PROGRESSIVE OVERLOAD

Each week, aim for:

- +1-2 reps at same weight on main lifts
- +2.5kg when you hit top of rep range
- Longer hold on stability exercises (when used as stimulus, not activation)
- Higher RPM or resistance on HIIT intervals

> Don't progress the activation work (dead bug, side plank, pull-aparts at start of session). Keep those at activation volume. Progress the main lifts.
