# EXERCISE GUIDE

## 90/90 Hip Switches

1. Sit on floor, one leg in front bent 90°, one leg to side bent 90°
1. Rotate hips to switch legs to opposite position
1. Keep chest tall throughout

## Thread the Needle

1. Start on all fours
1. Take one arm, reach under your body and rotate
1. Follow hand with eyes, feel thoracic stretch
1. Return and reach to ceiling

## Cable Pallof Press

1. Stand side-on to cable, handle at chest
1. Press straight out, hold 2 sec
1. Resist the rotation — core stays tight
1. Return to chest

## Half-Kneeling Cable Rotation

1. Kneel with inside knee down, outside foot forward
1. Hold cable handle at chest
1. Rotate torso away from cable
1. Hips stay completely still — only torso moves

## Goblet Squat to Rotation

1. Hold DB at chest, squat down
1. Stand up, rotate torso to one side
1. Return to centre, squat, rotate other side

## Slow Motion Swing Drill

1. Golf posture, arms crossed on chest
1. Backswing: rotate torso while keeping hips quiet
1. Pause at top
1. Downswing: hips fire first, then torso, then arms
1. Exaggerate the sequence — hips THEN torso THEN arms

|------------------------------|------------------------------------------------|

|Lean behind ball              |Single Leg RDL, Bulgarian Split Squat           |
